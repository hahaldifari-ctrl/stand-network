\# STAND Network — System Architecture Overview



\*\*Status:\*\* Foundational  

\*\*Version:\*\* 1.0  

\*\*Date:\*\* 2026-08-25



\---



\## 1. Vision



STAND Network is a Christian support and response platform designed to help persecuted and vulnerable Christian communities know that they are not standing alone.



The platform will begin with Nigeria and is designed to expand into other countries and regions experiencing persecution or faith-based violence.



STAND connects people who want to stand with affected communities through:



\- Prayer

\- Practical support

\- Emergency response

\- Giving

\- Volunteering

\- Partnerships

\- Advocacy

\- Storytelling

\- Long-term recovery



The central message of STAND is:



> \*\*You are not standing alone.\*\*



\---



\## 2. Mission



STAND exists to connect the wider Christian community with verified needs among persecuted and vulnerable Christians, while protecting the dignity, privacy, and safety of the people being served.



The platform seeks to move people from:



\*\*Awareness → Prayer → Compassion → Action → Recovery → Hope\*\*



\---



\## 3. The Problem



Christians facing persecution, displacement, violence, loss, and hardship can experience several forms of isolation.



\### Community isolation



Affected communities may feel forgotten by Christians outside their immediate region.



\### Information isolation



People who want to help may not know:



\- What happened

\- Where help is needed

\- Whether a report is genuine

\- What kind of help is required

\- Who is already responding



\### Response fragmentation



Churches, ministries, NGOs, donors, volunteers, and individuals may work independently without a shared coordination layer.



\### Trust challenges



Support platforms dealing with sensitive humanitarian situations must establish strong systems for:



\- Verification

\- Accountability

\- Financial transparency

\- Privacy

\- Security

\- Responsible storytelling



STAND is intended to provide a trusted coordination layer.
